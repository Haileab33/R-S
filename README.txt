R&S Restaurant website
1. Unzip this folder and double-click index.html (works in any browser, no internet needed except for fonts).
2. To show your friends online: drag this whole folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop (free) and share the link it gives you.
3. Photos live in the images folder. To change or add one, save a .jpg with the same name:
   breakfast-1..4  Ful, Fasting Firfir, Chechebsa, Enkulal Firfir
   lunch-1..7      Shiro Wot, Misir Wot, Vegetable Pasta, Lentil Soup, Doro Wot, Beef Tibs, Gomen Besiga
   dinner-1..4     Beyaynetu(have), Grilled Fish, Kitfo(have), Special Tibs(have)
   dessert-1..5    Fresh Fruit Plate, Baklava(have), Chocolate Cake(have), Ice Cream, Chocolate Trifle(have)
   warm-1..4       Buna, Shai, Macchiato, Hot Chocolate
   cold-1..4       Avocado Juice, Mango Juice, Milkshake, Yogurt Smoothie
   alcoholic-1..5  Tej(have), Draft Beer, House Red Wine, Irish Coffee, Signature Cocktail
   Dishes without a photo show a drawn picture until you add one.
4. Dishes, prices (ETB) and the fasting/non-fasting tags are examples: edit them in index.html at the top of the script (ITEMS).
